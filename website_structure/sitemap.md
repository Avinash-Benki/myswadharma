# mySwadharma Brand Strategy & Sitemap

## Brand Identity
**Vision:** To be the most trusted digital home for practicing Hindu families to connect with authentic traditions.
**Values:** Authenticity (Shastra-based), Trust (Verified providers), Serenity (Marble-clean design), and Continuity (Next-gen focus).
